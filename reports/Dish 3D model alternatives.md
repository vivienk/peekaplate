# Replace single-photo synthesis with truthful 3D

Testing the overhead kimchi dish (`03_kimchi`) on the official Hugging Face `microsoft/TRELLIS.2` Space at **`1536_cascade` in `bfloat16`** reproduces the same structural failure seen in PeekaPlate's Kaggle runs because **no single-image 3D diffusion model can recover vertical thickness, occluded undersides, or unentangled specular BRDFs from one `90°` top-down photo** ([dish-3d-delivery.md](file:///home/yacine105/t3-code-handoff/peekaplate/docs/research/dish-3d-delivery.md#L277-L300)). Across all five 3D and 2.5D paradigms audited against PeekaPlate's requirements, neither competing open-weights single-image models (`Hunyuan3D-2.1`, `Hi3DGen`, `Step1X-3D`, `TripoSG`, `SPAR3D`), commercial single-photo APIs (`Tripo3D v3.1`, `Hyper3D Rodin Gen-2`, `Meshy-7.1`, `Tencent Hunyuan3D Cloud`), nor single-image Gaussian splats (`apple/ml-sharp`, `TRELLIS-image-large`) resolve overhead depth ambiguity or pass [ADR 0004](file:///home/yacine105/t3-code-handoff/peekaplate/docs/adr/0004-visual-tiers.md#L11-L17)'s prohibition against invented edible surfaces (`publication_eligible: false`). ## Scaling TRELLIS.2 to 1536 bf16 preserves single-view blind spots on 03_kimchi

Stepping `microsoft/TRELLIS.2-4B` from PeekaPlate's Kaggle Version 31 baseline (`pipeline_type='512'`, `float32`, **`3,755` latent tokens**, `remesh_project=0.9`, `MARGIN=0.08`, `decimation_target=150000`) up to the official Hugging Face Space (`1536_cascade`, `bfloat16`, **`~30k–49k` sparse tokens** with `1024px` DINOv3 conditioning, `MARGIN=0`, `mesh.simplify(16777216)`, `decimation_target=300000`, `remesh_project=0`) eliminates Kaggle's vertex-snapping pinches without fixing any of the core failure modes on `03_kimchi` ([dish-3d-delivery.md](file:///home/yacine105/t3-code-handoff/peekaplate/docs/research/dish-3d-delivery.md#L283-L300)). Although exact `1536_cascade` `bf16` vertex and non-manifold counts on `03_kimchi` have not been recorded inside PeekaPlate's local instrumentation harness ([dish-3d-delivery.md](file:///home/yacine105/t3-code-handoff/peekaplate/docs/research/dish-3d-delivery.md#L303)), `TRELLIS.2`'s underlying architecture explains why repeated Space evaluations yield the same structural degradation across three coupled bottlenecks.

First, a steep overhead camera viewpoint mathematically eliminates vertical parallax before inference starts. PeekaPlate's `03_kimchi` cutout (`2000×1125` RGBA, **`32.66%` food foreground plus `11.25%` ceramic dish** segmented via `birefnet-general-lite` and `grounding_dino_sam`) views the plate from nearly `90°` overhead ([dish-3d-delivery.md](file:///home/yacine105/t3-code-handoff/peekaplate/docs/research/dish-3d-delivery.md#L144-L205); [dish-3d-delivery.md](file:///home/yacine105/t3-code-handoff/peekaplate/docs/research/dish-3d-delivery.md#L277)). Because `TRELLIS.2` synthesizes shapes inside a canonical `[-0.5, 0.5]^3` bounding box learned from object-centric 3D collections (`Objaverse-XL`, `ABO`, `3D-FUTURE`, `HSSD`), expanding sparse tokens from `3,755` to `~30k–49k` in `bfloat16` only refines the voxel discretization of the flow-matching DiT's *prior guess* rather than measuring missing `Y`-axis rim height, mound depth, or undersides ([arXiv:2512.14692](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.14692); [GitHub `microsoft/TRELLIS.2`](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQGIKP6gAJGMW4-0_42owTip9ZPLJW8PmoGRu9mlfxBzT4CsLsIXzIb-HQeGRI4DLqFvit25RzqletZsg0KedPSLz35mkVmymEYwq1ap6LZDvh_lCcQRAdr2LIeG)). Across Kaggle benchmarks, single-view underdetermination collapsed `Hunyuan3D-2mini` into thin wafers (`Z=0.176` on `03_kimchi` and `Z=0.049` on `01_pizza`), caused `TripoSR` to hallucinate a bulbous underside on `01_pizza`, and stretched `TRELLIS.2` V31 `03_kimchi` to `[0.999, 0.316, 0.873]` with an unverified synthetic base ([dish-3d-delivery.md](file:///home/yacine105/t3-code-handoff/peekaplate/docs/research/dish-3d-delivery.md#L153-L165); [dish-3d-delivery.md](file:///home/yacine105/t3-code-handoff/peekaplate/docs/research/dish-3d-delivery.md#L290)).

Second, the `16×` Sparse Compression VAE, Dual Contouring, and **`xatlas`** UV packing degrade fine texture across wet, curled cabbage foliage even at `1536_cascade`. `TRELLIS.2` encodes the image through `facebook/dinov3-vitl16-pretrain-lvd1689m`, compresses 3D structure by **`16×` spatially** into field-free O-Voxels on a Flexible Dual Grid, extracts geometry via Dual Contouring, decimates in `o_voxel.postprocess.to_glb`, and unwraps charts with `xatlas` into a `2048×2048` WebP PBR atlas ([arXiv:2512.14692](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.14692); [GitHub `microsoft/TRELLIS.2` `to_glb`](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQEE_imhyWZ9KDv4JAw0jLbFEMMxyJY1gGPNG36a76-LjLppzXsUzvXYzNcfzzchKHQwa_u-x69gPoMUnh1_hcFVSrDzRfW8XWNqLTLjBa9F4GmCAc0W-6R_8yXPrDShbFF7l2zH0VAXS2BXJsJmjfoh4p2d2HbFARsn7dpyAt75B5K6FcI=)). On Kaggle V31 (`remesh_project=0.9`), jagged chili-coated cabbage folds produced **`11,059` non-manifold edges** and a **`3.01×` vertex-split ratio** (`196,969` glTF vertices for `149,525` faces; `2,314,564`-byte mobile GLB; `+0.00462` signed volume), compared with just `143` non-manifold edges and a `1.42×` split ratio on smooth `04_gimbap` rolls ([dish-3d-delivery.md](file:///home/yacine105/t3-code-handoff/peekaplate/docs/research/dish-3d-delivery.md#L290-L291)). Setting `remesh_project=0` as in HF `app.py` trades projection-snap pinches for smoothed contours, yet the `16×` VAE latent bottleneck and fragmented `xatlas` chart gutters still dissipate texel density compared to the crisp `2000×1125` input photo ([dish-3d-delivery.md](file:///home/yacine105/t3-code-handoff/peekaplate/docs/research/dish-3d-delivery.md#L299-L300); [overhead_food_failure_and_multiview.md](file:///home/yacine105/t3-code-handoff/peekaplate/research_notes/Dish 3D model alternatives/overhead_food_failure_and_multiview.md#L31)).

Third, single-view O-Voxel `fmat` material prediction entangles glossy chili-oil reflections with geometry and reflectance. `TRELLIS.2` predicts `Base Color`, `Roughness`, `Metallic`, and `Opacity` in 3D directly from single-image DINOv3 features ([arXiv:2512.14692](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.14692); [GitHub `microsoft/TRELLIS.2`](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQFUoK7u22TvSdVFpRUUMpnLyWxCvnTHkwN543GTDqqPuXJVgqs39FBh07_tcWUW-ux31UBnKUtGI4aQT1u-ugr7odZaIZ1jtYhur91HMWwZu1eKqVVRg_Qclk3jKQ==)). Because BRDF and incident illumination cannot be separated from one photograph, specular wet sauces and oily leaves register as false albedo ridges, distorted roughness, or spurious metallic surfaces—matching PeekaPlate's documented rejection failure mode for *"Shiny sauces, steam and translucent food"* and quantified on `01_pizza` V20 at **`p95` metallic `0.85`** ([dish-3d-delivery.md](file:///home/yacine105/t3-code-handoff/peekaplate/docs/research/dish-3d-delivery.md#L71); [dish-3d-delivery.md](file:///home/yacine105/t3-code-handoff/peekaplate/docs/research/dish-3d-delivery.md#L275)).

## Five 3D paradigms trade topology, realism, and ADR 0004 legality

Evaluating whether any competing model or architecture solves `03_kimchi` requires comparing five distinct technical paradigms across geometric fidelity, material realism, hardware cost, payload weight, and [ADR 0004](file:///home/yacine105/t3-code-handoff/peekaplate/docs/adr/0004-visual-tiers.md#L11-L17) compliance.

### Open-weights single-image models split PBR quality from legal reach

Among open-weights alternatives to `TRELLIS.2-4B`, **`Hunyuan3D-2.1`** is the strongest technical challenger for glossy plated dishes because its decoupled two-stage pipeline pairs a `~3.3B` watertight SDF Diffusion Transformer (`Hunyuan3D-DiT-v2-1`, **`~10–11.5 GB` VRAM**) with `Hunyuan3D-PaintPBR-v2-1` (`~2B` parameters in paper summaries, `~1.3B` in one secondary summary, **`~21 GB` VRAM** across up to `6` views at `512px`, **`~24.5–29 GB` combined**), separating Disney Principled BRDF **`Albedo`**, **`Metallic-Roughness`**, and **`Normal`** maps ([Tencent-Hunyuan/Hunyuan3D-2.1](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hunyuan3D-2.1); [arXiv:2506.15442](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.15442)). That separation prevents `TRELLIS.2`'s chili-oil highlight baking in principle and avoids `Hunyuan3D-2mini`'s geometry-only (`0.6B`, `4.08 GiB` T4) flatness, while `Hunyuan3D-2.5` (`10B` LATTICE) and `3.0` remain closed hosted APIs ([arXiv:2506.16504](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.16504); [dish-3d-delivery.md](file:///home/yacine105/t3-code-handoff/peekaplate/docs/research/dish-3d-delivery.md#L106-L160)). However, `Hunyuan3D-2.1` cannot fit `PaintPBR` inside a `15 GB` Tesla T4 without CPU offloading (`--low_vram_mode`) and its **Tencent Hunyuan 3D Community License** explicitly excludes the **European Union, United Kingdom, and South Korea** alongside a **1 million MAU** cap ([Tencent-Hunyuan/Hunyuan3D-2.1](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hunyuan3D-2.1); [tencent/Hunyuan3D-2.1](https://huggingface.co/tencent/Hunyuan3D-2.1)).

Every MIT or Apache-2.0 challenger trades away either native PBR materials, hardware compatibility, or segmentation cleanliness. **`Hi3DGen`** (`Stable-X/Stable3DGen`, **MIT**, **`24 GB` VRAM** recommended) replaces RGB conditioning with a **`NiRNE` normal-bridging estimator** and `NoRLD` normal-regularized TRELLIS latent diffusion trained on `~700k` `DetailVerse` assets with proprietary NVIDIA `kaolin`/`nvdiffrast`/`flexicubes` stripped out—sharpening geometric ridges on layered dishes, though remaining **geometry-only** and susceptible to reading sharp wet-oil specular edges as false normal creases unless paired with a separate PBR painter ([Stable-X/Stable3DGen](https://github.com/Stable-X/Stable3DGen); [Stable-X/trellis-normal-v0-1](https://huggingface.co/Stable-X/trellis-normal-v0-1)). **`TripoSG`** (`VAST-AI-Research/TripoSG`, **MIT**, `1.5B` rectified-flow SDF DiT at `2048` latent tokens, **`>=8 GB` VRAM** in `FP16`) fits easily on a `15 GB` T4 and dramatically sharpens geometry over `TripoSR` (`2.323 GiB`, vertex colors only), yet is likewise **geometry-only**, defaults to non-commercial **`briaai/RMBG-1.4`** unless swapped for PeekaPlate's plate-preserving cutout, and requires pairing with external Apache-2.0 texturers such as **`MV-Adapter`** and **`TEXGen`** ([VAST-AI-Research/TripoSG](https://github.com/VAST-AI-Research/TripoSG); [huanngzh/MV-Adapter](https://huggingface.co/huanngzh/MV-Adapter); [Andyx/TEXGen](https://huggingface.co/Andyx/TEXGen)). **`Step1X-3D`** (`stepfun-ai/Step1X-3D`, **Apache-2.0** repo, `1.3B` TSDF DiT + SDXL texture) consumes **`~27–29 GB` VRAM** (`~152s`), synthesizes RGB/albedo-aligned textures without full metallic/roughness PBR maps, and depends on **SDXL** (`CreativeML Open RAIL++-M`) ([stepfun-ai/Step1X-3D](https://github.com/stepfun-ai/Step1X-3D)). Secondary contenders fare worse on PeekaPlate's constraints: **`Direct3D-S2`** (**MIT**, up to `2B`, `1024^3` sparse SDF at `~24 GB` VRAM) and **`TripoSF`** (**MIT**, `>=12 GB`, `1024^3`) are geometry-only—with `Direct3D-S2` requiring Ampere+ GPUs for FlashAttention and failing on T4 `sm_75` ([DreamTechAI/Direct3D-S2](https://github.com/DreamTechAI/Direct3D-S2); [VAST-AI-Research/TripoSF](https://github.com/VAST-AI-Research/TripoSF)); **`Unique3D`** (**MIT**, `~12–16 GB+`) outputs RGB/vertex textures without full PBR ([AiuniAI/Unique3D](https://github.com/AiuniAI/Unique3D)); **`SPAR3D`** (`~10.5 GB` / `~7 GB` low-VRAM) and **`SF3D`** (`~6 GB`) predict fast PBR textures but impose Stability AI's **`$1M` annual gross revenue threshold** ([stabilityai/stable-point-aware-3d](https://huggingface.co/stabilityai/stable-point-aware-3d); [Stability-AI/stable-fast-3d](https://github.com/Stability-AI/stable-fast-3d)); and **`CraftsMan3D`** is geometry-only under **AGPL-3.0** network copyleft ([wyysf-98/CraftsMan](https://github.com/wyysf-98/CraftsMan)).

## Conclusion

The repeated HF Space degradation on `03_kimchi` settles a lingering technical question: PeekaPlate was never one GPU tier or `remesh_project` flag away from viable single-photo 3D dish reconstruction. Running `TRELLIS.2-4B` at `1536_cascade` `bfloat16` sharpens voxel discretization around an underdetermined projection while preserving overhead thickness ambiguity, `16×` VAE and `xatlas` seam losses across curled chili cabbage, and specular highlight baking—and every alternative single-image mesh or 3DGS generator runs into the same geometry limit, [ADR 0004](file:///home/yacine105/t3-code-handoff/peekaplate/docs/adr/0004-visual-tiers.md#L17)'s anti-hallucination boundary, or disqualifying license and payload barriers.

Separating **photorealistic parallax presentation** from **true spatial 3D capture** clears both engineering lanes immediately. Single-photo menu dishes could be evaluated for depth tilt and ceramic framing via `MoGe-2` projective relief without fabricating a single ingredient pixel, while true multi-angle diner inspection transitions onto a lightweight 4-photo capture workflow driven by sparse-view geometric foundation models rather than single-image object diffusion.
