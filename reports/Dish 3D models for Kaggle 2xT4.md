# Dish 3D models for Kaggle 2xT4

2026-11-23

## Decision and evidence boundary

**No model identified here is demonstrated to beat TRELLIS.2-4B on plated food while producing a textured GLB on this Kaggle configuration.** A VRAM figure alone does not prove CUDA 12.8, Turing, or the complete export works. The shortlist below is a priority order for experiments, not a ranking of proven quality. This is a partial survey: quantitative cross-model metrics, and several requested candidates, remain unverified below. All runtimes and hardware claims apply only to the configurations identified in their sources.

PeekaPlate's own 14-dish TRELLIS.2 run measured roughly 5–6 min/dish, median 293k faces, 2048-pixel textures, two welded boundary edges and 11.5 shells; non-manifold edges remain, and two examples contain a ground slab. See [local run profile](../research_notes/wiki/trellis-run-profile.md) and [delivery measurements](../docs/research/dish-3d-delivery.md). Two 16 GB cards do not automatically make 32 GB available to one model. Generated unseen edible surfaces cannot be published under [ADR 0004](../docs/adr/0004-visual-tiers.md); these experiments are private drafts.

## Ranked experiments

1. **SPAR3D — best practical textured comparator, quality unproven.** The [official repository](https://github.com/Stability-AI/stable-point-aware-3d) describes GLB output, improved backside inference over SF3D and 10.5 GB default or roughly 7 GB low-VRAM use. Its [model card](https://huggingface.co/stabilityai/stable-point-aware-3d) describes materials and the Stability community license. This is a plausible memory fit, not evidence of T4/CUDA 12.8 operation or quality parity with TRELLIS.2. Proposed key `spar3d`: first compile its texture baker on sm_75, run a single plate, record peak VRAM and end-to-end time; then compare geometry and textures blind against the same baseline dishes. The license is gated and commercial use depends on the [community license terms](https://stability.ai/license); check revenue and attribution provisions. No sourced T4 time or head-to-head TRELLIS.2 metric verified.
2. **Step1X-3D — quality-oriented, hardware feasibility unproven.** The [authors' repository](https://github.com/stepfun-ai/Step1X-3D) releases geometry and texture weights and states Apache 2.0; its texture pipeline also has separately licensed dependencies. Existing [research](../research_notes/Dish%203D%20model%20alternatives/open_weights_3d_models.md) reports approximately 27–29 GB combined and calls for staged offload; that configuration and sm_75/fp16 compatibility are not independently verified here. Proposed key `step1x-3d`: inspect the two stages and dependencies, then test sequential unloading and CPU offload on one dish before committing GPU quota. Full textured pipeline, CUDA extensions and RAM are installation risks. No T4 timing or comparable TRELLIS.2 result verified.
3. **Hunyuan3D-2.1 — conditional experiment only.** Its [official repository](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hunyuan3D-2.1) provides shape and PBR paint; the existing [research](../research_notes/Dish%203D%20model%20alternatives/open_weights_3d_models.md) cites approximately 10 GB shape and 21 GB paint. Two GPUs do not resolve the per-device paint requirement without a working split or offload. Proposed key `hunyuan3d-21`: defer to the separately commissioned [feasibility note](../research_notes/wiki/hunyuan3d-21-feasibility.md) when available; test paint on T4 only if that assessment identifies a viable path. Its [license](https://huggingface.co/tencent/Hunyuan3D-2.1/blob/main/LICENSE.txt) has territorial and commercial conditions; verify applicability before deployment. T4 full-pipeline time and quality versus TRELLIS.2 remain unverified.

Keys are proposed identifiers, not existing implementations. No notebook or Kaggle jobs were changed.

## Follow-up evidence, 2026-11-23

- **Step1X memory:** the [official inference instructions](https://github.com/stepfun-ai/Step1X-3D#5-inference-script) report 27 GB for geometry plus texture and 29 GB for the labeled geometry variant plus texture, both at 50 steps. They show sequential stages but do not give separate measured peaks or establish that texture fits 16 GB with documented options. The [installation](https://github.com/stepfun-ai/Step1X-3D#4-dependencies-and-installation) was checked by the authors on CUDA 12.4, uses PyTorch3D, Kaolin and two custom renderer builds; CUDA 12.8, T4 fp16 and offload remain unverified.
- **SPAR3D build:** its [texture baker setup](https://github.com/Stability-AI/stable-point-aware-3d/blob/main/texture_baker/setup.py) chooses a PyTorch CUDA extension when CUDA is available; its [UV unwrapper setup](https://github.com/Stability-AI/stable-point-aware-3d/blob/main/uv_unwrapper/setup.py) uses a C++ extension. Neither setup file establishes a successful CUDA 12.8 / sm_75 build. No successful build or relevant issue was verified; compiling and executing the full pipeline remains necessary.
- **Independent comparison:** [185-Hard](https://huggingface.co/datasets/molodata/185-hard-image-to-3D-eval-dataset) evaluates image-to-3D systems on 185 difficult scenarios using VLM-based scoring; its leaderboard reports Step1X-3D overall 2.70 versus TRELLIS 2.53. These are the benchmark authors' scores, not a TRELLIS.2 comparison or a T4 feasibility measurement. The [TRELLIS.2 paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/papers/Xiang_Native_and_Compact_Structured_Latents_for_3D_Generation_CVPR_2026_paper.pdf), table 2, also compares several models, but the dataset and table values need a careful independent transcription before using them here.
- **Budget for a first experiment:** Prioritize `spar3d` as a proposed new model key, contingent on a single-dish build and run. A full 41-image GPU-hour estimate is **unverified** until that probe yields T4 timings; baseline TRELLIS.2 alone extrapolates to about 3.4–4.1 GPU-hours at the [measured 5–6 minutes per dish](../research_notes/wiki/trellis-run-profile.md), excluding setup and failures. `hunyuan3d-21` is a possible second proposed key for shape plus existing photographic projection, estimated 1.5–3 GPU-hours for 41 images by the [feasibility note](../research_notes/wiki/hunyuan3d-21-feasibility.md); that is not its native PBR pipeline. Neither key is currently implemented in the [notebook builder](../processing/scripts/build_multi_model_notebooks.py).

**Research still incomplete:** the individual model cards, requirements, issue histories, arXiv comparisons, later releases, and the remaining candidate-by-candidate compatibility and license matrix have not all been verified. No inference about the absence of a better feasible model follows from this partial search.
