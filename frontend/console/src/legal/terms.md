## What is PeekaPlate?

PeekaPlate is a menu tool for restaurants. You can:

- **Publish a menu.** Diners open it on their phone from a link.
- **See how diners use it.** For example, which dishes were viewed or compared.
- **Upload order files (optional).** So we can later connect menu activity to what was ordered.

PeekaPlate is a **pilot**. We may limit it to selected restaurants.

## What PeekaPlate is NOT

- **It does not check your allergens or ingredients.** You are responsible for what you publish. PeekaPlate does not replace your own food-safety procedures.
- **It is not proof that a menu change caused a change in sales.** We do not promise more revenue.
- **It is not a point-of-sale or accounting system.** We do not connect to your POS in this pilot.
- **It is not guaranteed to be always on or error-free.** Reports can be delayed, incomplete, or wrong.

## Your menu and your files

"Your Content" means your menus, dish names and descriptions, ingredient and allergen information, photos, and order files.

- **You own it.** We do not.
- **We can use it only to run the service for you.** That means publishing your menu, building your reports, and keeping the service secure.
- **Publishing makes a menu public to anyone with its link.** Do not put anything in a menu you do not want diners to see.
- **You must have the right to give it to us,** and it must not break anyone's rights.

## Order files

Uploading order files is covered by the Pilot Order-Outcome Data Agreement. You accept that agreement in the console first. Uploads are blocked until you do.

- Upload only the approved columns.
- **Never include diner personal data,** such as names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, payment details, or loyalty IDs. Files with extra columns are rejected.
- Orders older than **90 days** at upload are not imported.
- During the pilot we keep the original file with **no automatic expiry,** until you ask us to delete it. We review this after owner testing and tell you if it changes.

## Diner activity

When diners open your published menu and agree, we record menu activity such as menu views and dish views, to build your reports.

- We keep a visit identifier and event identifiers. We do **not** link them to a named person, and we do not collect diner names, emails, or phone numbers in this data.
- Because those identifiers exist, we do not call this data completely anonymous.
- **You agree not to try to identify individual diners** from our reports or data.

## What your reports mean

- Reports show **patterns** in menu activity.
- We **hide results with too little activity** (fewer than 10 sessions) to protect diners.
- Order data is not shown in reports until its matching rules are approved.
- Use your own judgment before you change your menu.

- **Order data:** ask us at hello@peekaplate.io or in the console. We confirm receipt within **5 business days** and delete live data within **30 days**. One named person runs the deletion and a second person checks it.
- **Diner activity data for your restaurant:** ask us, and we delete it by restaurant or by time window.
- **Honest limits on deleting:**

- **Closing your account:** this does not delete your data automatically. We will tell you that you can export your data or ask for deletion. If we hear nothing for **90 days,** we delete your order data.
