# Privacy Policy

*PeekaPlate for restaurant owners*

This page explains what information PeekaPlate collects from restaurant owners and staff, what we do with it, who sees it, and what you can ask us to do. Our **Terms and Conditions** cover the rules for using PeekaPlate. The notice shown to diners on a menu is separate.

**Last updated:** November 14, 2026

---

## The short version

- We collect what we need to run your account, publish your menu, and build your reports.
- Your menu and order files are yours. We use them only to run PeekaPlate for you.
- We never sell your information or use it for ads.
- Diner activity is recorded with visit and event identifiers, not names or contact details.
- You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it.
- You can stop and close your account at any time.

The rest of this page explains all of that. Take your time. You can ask us questions before you decide.

## Who we are

PeekaPlate ("we", "us") runs the PeekaPlate console and the diner menu service. You can reach us at [contact email].

## What we ask you for

**Things we need:**
- Your email address and sign-in details, so you can log in.
- Your restaurant name, and who has access to it and in what role.
- Your menu: dish names, descriptions, ingredients, allergens, prices, and images.

**Things you can choose to give us:**
- **Order files.** CSV files with order ID, order line ID, order date and time, outcome type, quantity, item identifiers, and optionally price and currency.
- Messages and feedback you send us.

**Things we record on our own:**
- When you agreed to our documents, and which version.
- Sign-in events, IP address, browser and device type, and logs, for security and to fix problems.
- Any deletion request you make.

**Things we never ask for:**
- **Diner personal data in your order files** (names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, payment details, loyalty IDs). Files with extra columns are rejected, and we check order identifiers for things that look like emails or long numbers.
- Payment card details. PeekaPlate does not take payments.
- Government ID numbers.

We do not knowingly collect information from children, and PeekaPlate is not made for them.

## What we record about diners for your restaurant

When a diner opens your published menu and agrees, we record menu activity such as menu views, dish views, and comparisons, with the time and menu version.

- We keep a **visit identifier and event identifiers.** We do not link them to a named person, and we do not collect diner names, emails, or phone numbers in this data.
- Because the identifiers exist, we do **not** describe this data as completely anonymous.
- The notice shown to diners on the menu explains this to them.

## How we use your information

We use it to:
- create and secure your account, and confirm who may act for your restaurant;
- publish your menu and build your reports;
- process order files you upload, for the purposes in your Pilot Order-Outcome Data Agreement: re-processing after fixes, owner reporting, and the planned order conversion metric once its rules are approved;
- delete data when you ask, and keep a record that we did;
- keep the service safe and fix errors;
- contact you about your account and changes to our terms;
- meet legal obligations.

## Who can see your information

- **You,** and staff you add. You see only your own restaurant's reports.
- **A small number of PeekaPlate staff,** only as needed to run the service, fix problems, or carry out a deletion you asked for. Deletions are run by one named person and checked by a second.
- **Our service providers,** under contracts that limit what they can do:

| Provider | What it does | What it may hold |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Supabase | Database and sign-in | Account details, restaurant data, order records, report facts |
| Cloudflare | Hosting, file storage and networking | Menu files, uploaded order files, archives, request logs |
| PostHog (US) | Product analytics for diner activity | Diner menu activity events, no diner names or contact details. Not the source of your reports. |

Your information is stored in the **United States.**

- **Other people, only if the law requires,** to protect safety, or if the business is sold or merged (we will tell you).
- **Other restaurants never see your data.** Each restaurant's data is kept separate. Reports with too little activity (fewer than 10 sessions) are hidden.

## What we will never do

- **We will never sell your information.** Not to companies, not to data brokers, not to anyone.
- **We will never use it for advertising.**
- **We will never use your menu or order files for marketing,** unrelated products, analytics unrelated to your reports, or training AI models.
- **We will never try to work out who an individual diner is.**

## Being honest about the law

If a court or the law requires us to hand over data, we have to follow it. We cannot promise this will never happen.

What we do promise:
- We will push back on any request that is too broad or not legally required.
- We will tell you if we are allowed to.
- We keep as little as possible, so there is as little as possible to hand over.

## How long we keep things

| What | How long |
| --- | --- |
| Account and restaurant details | While your account is open |
| Original uploaded order file | **No automatic expiry in the pilot** (reviewed after owner testing), or until you ask us to delete it |
| Imported order records | Only orders within **90 days** of upload are imported. Kept until you ask us to delete them |
| Diner activity events | **13 months** from the event time |
| Report totals | While your account is open |
| Events waiting on a diner's phone | Up to 24 hours, or cleared at once if the diner withdraws |
| Deletion and agreement records | Kept as a record. They hold request and restaurant IDs and counts, never your order contents or diner data |
| Backups | Expire on the provider's normal schedule |
| PostHog copy of diner events | PostHog's plan keeps events about 1 year. Our own copies follow the 13-month rule |

After the time is up, we delete the data.

## Your rights

You can:
- **See your data.** Ask for a copy of what we hold about you and your restaurant.
- **Fix your data.** If something is wrong, tell us and we will correct it.
- **Delete order data.** We confirm receipt within **5 business days** and delete live data within **30 days.**
- **Delete diner activity data for your restaurant.** By restaurant or by time window.
- **Close your account.** We tell you that you can export your data or ask for deletion. If we hear nothing for **90 days,** we delete your order data.
- **Stop.** You do not need to explain.

To ask, email [contact email]. We may need to check that you are an authorized owner first.

**Honest limits on deleting:**
- Our database backups cannot be edited for one restaurant, so backup copies expire on the provider's normal schedule.
- We cannot delete data for one individual diner, because we cannot tell who they are.
- If your restaurant's activity was already counted in report totals, those totals stay while your account is open.

### If you are a California resident

California law may give you rights to know, correct, and delete your personal information, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We do not sell or share personal information for advertising. We handle your requests as described on this page.

We follow California's online privacy rules. We do not track you across other websites. Because there is no common standard for Do Not Track signals, we do not respond to them.

## Cookies

The console uses cookies or local storage to keep you signed in and secure.

## Keeping your information safe

We protect it with encryption, access limited to people who need it, separate storage for each restaurant, and a two-person check on every deletion.

We will not claim it is impossible to break in. No system is. If there is a data breach affecting you, we will tell you promptly what happened, what was involved, and what you can do.

## We may change this page

If we change something important, we will tell you at least 14 days before it takes effect, and explain what changed in plain words, not just a new date at the top. If you do not agree, you can close your account and ask us to delete your data.
