# Why Single-Photo 3D Fails on Overhead Dish Photos (`03_kimchi`) & Evaluation of Multi-View Capture vs. Multi-View Diffusion Under ADR 0004

## Root Causes of Single-Photo Failure on `03_kimchi` (Even at `1536_cascade` `bf16`)

### Takeaway
Scaling `microsoft/TRELLIS.2-4B` from `512` FP32 (`3,755` latent tokens) to the official Hugging Face Space `1536_cascade` `bf16` (`~30k–49k` sparse tokens + `1024px` DINOv3 conditioning, `remesh_project=0`, `decimation_target=300000`) sharpens voxel discretization and avoids V31's projection-pinch artifacts, but cannot resolve three structural bottlenecks on `03_kimchi`: mathematically undetermined vertical depth and undersides from a steep overhead photo, `16×` VAE compression + Dual Contouring/`xatlas` degradation across thin wet cabbage folds and chili-oil crevices, and single-view specular/albedo/material entanglement.

#### Bottleneck 1: Geometric Depth & Underside Ill-Posedness from High-Elevation Views (`65°–90°` Overhead)

- **Canonical normalized generation box & large-scale 3D priors:** `TRELLIS.2` generates shapes in a canonical `[-0.5, 0.5]^3` bounding box (`aabb=[[-0.5, -0.5, -0.5], [0.5, 0.5, 0.5]]`) trained on large-scale public 3D object collections including Objaverse-XL, ABO, 3D-FUTURE, and HSSD — [arXiv:2512.14692](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.14692) and [GitHub `microsoft/TRELLIS.2`](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQGIKP6gAJGMW4-0_42owTip9ZPLJW8PmoGRu9mlfxBzT4CsLsIXzIb-HQeGRI4DLqFvit25RzqletZsg0KedPSLz35mkVmymEYwq1ap6LZDvh_lCcQRAdr2LIeG).

#### Bottleneck 2: High-Frequency Organic Food Topology vs. DINOv3 + `16×` O-Voxel Dual Contouring & `xatlas`
- **Architecture chain:** `TRELLIS.2` encodes the cutout image via `facebook/dinov3-vitl16-pretrain-lvd1689m` (`1024px` conditioning in `1536_cascade`), runs a 4B-parameter flow-matching DiT over a Sparse Compression VAE with `16×` spatial downsampling into field-free O-Voxels on a Flexible Dual Grid, extracts geometry via Dual Contouring (<100 ms CUDA), simplifies/remeshes (`o_voxel.postprocess.to_glb`), and performs UV unwrapping (`xatlas`) to bake `2048×2048` PBR maps (WebP in GLB) — [arXiv:2512.14692](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.14692), [dish-3d-delivery.md](file:///home/yacine105/t3-code-handoff/peekaplate/docs/research/dish-3d-delivery.md#L278-L300), and [GitHub `microsoft/TRELLIS.2` `to_glb`](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQEE_imhyWZ9KDv4JAw0jLbFEMMxyJY1gGPNG36a76-LjLppzXsUzvXYzNcfzzchKHQwa_u-x69gPoMUnh1_hcFVSrDzRfW8XWNqLTLjBa9F4GmCAc0W-6R_8yXPrDShbFF7l2zH0VAXS2BXJsJmjfoh4p2d2HbFARsn7dpyAt75B5K6FcI=).
- **Empirical topology stress on `03_kimchi`:** Even with remeshing enabled in V31, `03_kimchi` (curled napa cabbage margins + dark crevices + scalloped chili dish) suffered `11,059` non-manifold edges and a `3.01×` vertex split ratio (`196,969` glTF vertices for `149,525` faces), whereas simpler rolled shapes (`04_gimbap`) had only `143` non-manifold edges and a `1.42×` split ratio — [dish-3d-delivery.md](file:///home/yacine105/t3-code-handoff/peekaplate/docs/research/dish-3d-delivery.md#L290-L291).

#### Bottleneck 3: Canonical Object Pose & Specular Material Entanglement
- **Native 3D PBR prediction from a single photo:** `TRELLIS.2`'s O-Voxel `fmat` branch predicts Base Color, Roughness, Metallic, and Opacity directly in 3D space from single-image `DINOv3` conditioning — [arXiv:2512.14692](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.14692) and [GitHub `microsoft/TRELLIS.2`](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQFUoK7u22TvSdVFpRUUMpnLyWxCvnTHkwN543GTDqqPuXJVgqs39FBh07_tcWUW-ux31UBnKUtGI4aQT1u-ugr7odZaIZ1jtYhur91HMWwZu1eKqVVRg_Qclk3jKQ==).
- **Observed shiny/reflective failure risk:** PeekaPlate's delivery research explicitly flags *"Shiny sauces, steam and translucent food"* as primary rejection failure modes, and in V20 `01_pizza` exhibited `p95` metallic `0.85` baked from background/lighting reflections — [dish-3d-delivery.md](file:///home/yacine105/t3-code-handoff/peekaplate/docs/research/dish-3d-delivery.md#L71) and [dish-3d-delivery.md](file:///home/yacine105/t3-code-handoff/peekaplate/docs/research/dish-3d-delivery.md#L275).

### Inferences
- **Why `1536_cascade` `bf16` cannot fix Bottleneck 1 (ill-posed depth):** A single `90°` projection mathematically eliminates vertical `Y` parallax (plate rim height, foot-ring undercut, kimchi mound profile, and undersides). Increasing sparse token count (`3,755` at `512` -> `~30k–49k` at `1536_cascade`) and changing tensor precision (`fp32` -> `bf16`) only refines the voxel discretization of the DiT's *prior guess* in `[-0.5, 0.5]^3`, leaving the model guessing between a flat relief and a thick boulder/wedge or mirroring top textures underneath.
- **Why `1536_cascade` `bf16` + `xatlas` still struggles with Bottleneck 2:** Even at `1536_cascade`, the Sparse Compression VAE applies `16×` spatial downsampling before O-Voxel Dual Contouring, and `o_voxel.postprocess.to_glb` simplifies/decimates (`300k` target in `app.py`) before `xatlas` packs fragmented UV charts into a single `2048×2048` atlas. On jagged, self-overlapping wet cabbage folds (`3.01×` vertex split ratio in V31), UV chart boundaries and gutter padding dissipate texel density compared to the crisp `2000×1125` 2D photo, while `remesh_project=0` (in `app.py`) trades V31's `11,059` projection-snap pinches for softer/smoothed leaf contours.
- **Why `1536_cascade` `bf16` cannot fix Bottleneck 3:** Because reflectance (`BRDF`) and illumination cannot be uniquely separated from one RGB frame, bright specular highlights on wet chili oil or glossy plates get interpreted by `DINOv3` + O-Voxel `fmat` as bright diffuse ridges, low roughness, or spurious metallic values, while non-canonical steep overhead angles deprive the model of side-horizon cues needed to orient the plate reliably.

## Paradigm Fix A — Real Multi-View Capture (2–4 Phone Photos) -> Multi-View 3D Models

### Takeaway
For 2–4 real phone photos, **multi-view generative conditioners** (`Hunyuan3D-2mv`, `Tripo multiview_to_model`, `Meshy`) expect `0°`-elevation orthogonal turnarounds and still hallucinate unseen edible surfaces (failing ADR 0004 publication rules), whereas **feed-forward sparse-view geometry models** (`MASt3R` / `VGGT` paired with `2DGS`) can solve unposed wide-baseline camera poses and surface depth from **3–5 overlapping photos** (1 high-angle `~60–75°` hero shot + 2–3 low-elevation `~20–35°` rim/profile shots), provided orbit angles remain clamped to observed tabletop coverage per ADR 0004.

- **PeekaPlate ADR 0004 hard lines:** Prohibits *"invented edible surfaces or uncalibrated 'actual size' claim"*; physical dimensions require a measured plate reference, and model/checkpoint licenses must be verified before processing captures (note `TRELLIS.2` depends on gated `Meta DINOv3` and `BRIA RMBG-2.0`) — [ADR 0004](file:///home/yacine105/t3-code-handoff/peekaplate/docs/adr/0004-visual-tiers.md#L17) and [dish-3d-delivery.md](file:///home/yacine105/t3-code-handoff/peekaplate/docs/research/dish-3d-delivery.md#L73).
- **Capture & publication rules in `dish-3d-delivery.md`:** Requires capturing *"the same undisturbed dish from overlapping angles at two elevations, under even light, with a fixed lens/zoom"*, recording a sharp hero frame for T0/T1, inspecting against held-out source views, requiring **captured texture provenance**, and **preserving gaps or restricting orbit view range** when coverage is incomplete — [dish-3d-delivery.md](file:///home/yacine105/t3-code-handoff/peekaplate/docs/research/dish-3d-delivery.md#L39-L46) and [dish-3d-delivery.md](file:///home/yacine105/t3-code-handoff/peekaplate/docs/research/dish-3d-delivery.md#L71).

### Inferences
- **Practical photo budget for a restaurant owner:**
  - **2 photos** (1 `~65–75°` hero + 1 `~25°` front shot) are enough to constrain visible front plate thickness in human review, *but* leave the rear 180° of heaped dishes unobserved and fail 360° triangulation.
  - **4 photos minimum (`1` top-down/high-angle `~60–75°` hero shot + `3` low-elevation `~20–35°` shots spaced ~`120°` around the plate under diffuse light)** represent the practical minimum capture set: they observe the plate rim, foot-ring clearance, and mound silhouette from every azimuth, giving `VGGT` / `MASt3R` + `2DGS` sufficient cross-elevation overlap to solve `Y` depth without hallucinating plate thickness.
  - **Why `Hunyuan3D-2mv` / `Tripo` / `Meshy` clash with this 4-photo restaurant flow:** Restaurant captures pair a `60–75°` hero menu shot with lower rim angles, whereas `Hunyuan3D-2mv`, `Tripo`, and `Meshy` expect `0°`-elevation `front/left/back/right` plates; moreover, because they synthesize surfaces via diffusion priors rather than projecting strictly captured pixels onto triangulated geometry, their output remains `publication_eligible: false` under ADR 0004.
  - **Why `MASt3R` / `VGGT` + `2DGS` + bounded tabletop orbit aligns with ADR 0004:** A dish resting on a table never reveals its underside (`Y < 0`); clamping the Three.js diner orbit camera above the plate rim (`dish-3d-delivery.md:39,46`) eliminates any need to invent underside geometry while baking captured pixels onto `VGGT`/`MASt3R` + `2DGS` reconstructed surfaces.

### Gaps
- No PeekaPlate dish (`01_pizza`–`04_gimbap`) has yet been captured from multiple elevations and benchmarked on `Hunyuan3D-2mv`, `TRELLIS` `run_multi_image`, `MASt3R` + `2DGS`, or `VGGT` + `2DGS` (`dish-3d-delivery.md:247,303`).
- Primary documentation for `Hunyuan3D-2mv`, `Tripo`, and `Meshy` did not state exact failure tolerances (in degrees) when mixing a `60–75°` top-down hero shot with `20–35°` side shots.
- Primary quantitative accuracy metrics comparing `MASt3R` + `2DGS` vs. `VGGT` + `2DGS` specifically on `3–4` views of shiny/wet food were not found in the searched papers.

## Paradigm Fix B — Synthetic Single-Image -> Multi-View Consistent Diffusion -> Multi-View 3D

### Takeaway
Two-step `Photo -> 4–16 synthetic canonical views -> Multi-View 3D` pipelines (`Zero123++`, `SyncDreamer`, `SV3D`, `Era3D`, `MV-Adapter`, `Unique3D`, `One-2-3-45++`) can convert a single image into consistent side/back turnarounds before meshing, and elevation-adaptive designs (`Era3D`, `MV-Adapter`, `SV3D_p`) reduce camera-mismatch warping compared to fixed-elevation models (`Zero123++`, `SyncDreamer`); however, on an overhead `03_kimchi` photo they still invent side profiles and back-side ingredients from 2D priors, strictly barring them from published PeekaPlate menus (`publication_eligible: false`) under ADR 0004.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- **Does Paradigm B fix `03_kimchi`'s boulder/underside bug?**
  - **Models with rigid low-elevation priors (`SyncDreamer` expecting `[-10°, 40°]` input, `Zero123++` fixed `20°/-10°`, `Unique3D` horizontal orthos)** fail or warp on steep `65°–90°` overhead inputs due to camera-prior mismatch, often hallucinating upright objects or tilted slabs.
  - **Elevation-aware pipelines (`Era3D`, `MV-Adapter`, `SV3D_p`, `One-2-3-45++`)** can synthesize visually plausible side silhouettes and reduce extreme flat-wafer collapse when feeding Stage-2 3D reconstructors (like `Hunyuan3D-2mv` or `TRELLIS` `run_multi_image`), *but* they cannot resolve the true height of the kimchi mound, plate rim depth, foot-ring, or back-side ingredients because that geometric information does not exist in a single `90°` image. Any generated side or rear view is pure 2D-diffusion hallucination (creating plausible-looking generic bowls and invented cabbage/garnish placement).
- **Strict PeekaPlate ADR 0004 boundary:** Synthetic multi-view diffusion (and 2D/VLM novel-view image edits) may be used **only** to improve private aesthetic mockups or pipeline testing (`publication_eligible: false`) and **never** for published diner menus (`publication_eligible: true`), which require captured multi-elevation imagery.

### Gaps
- Direct quantitative benchmarks comparing `MV-Adapter`, `Era3D`, `Unique3D`, `SV3D`, `Zero123++`, and `One-2-3-45++` specifically on steep `65°–90°` overhead food photos were not found in the searched primary literature.
- PeekaPlate has not run a local `03_kimchi -> Era3D / MV-Adapter -> Hunyuan3D-2mv / TRELLIS-MV` private mockup experiment to measure geometric/visual deltas against V31.
