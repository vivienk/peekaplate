# Open-Weights Single-Image and Multi-View 3D Mesh + PBR Models vs. Microsoft TRELLIS.2-4B (2025–2026)

## 1. Tencent Hunyuan3D-2.1, Hunyuan3D-2 / 2mv, and Hunyuan3D-2.5 / 3.0 vs. TRELLIS.2-4B

### Takeaway
`Hunyuan3D-2.1` is the strongest open-weights native PBR challenger to `TRELLIS.2-4B` for glossy, layered food photos because its decoupled `Hunyuan3D-PaintPBR-v2-1` pipeline explicitly predicts Disney Principled BRDF `Albedo`, `Metallic-Roughness`, and `Normal` maps (`~21 GB` VRAM for paint alone, `~29 GB` combined), whereas `Hunyuan3D-2mini` tested in PeekaPlate is a `0.6B` geometry-only fast variant (`4.08 GiB` VRAM). However, `Hunyuan3D-2.1` (`21–29 GB` VRAM) cannot fit `PaintPBR` natively into a single `15 GB` Tesla T4 without aggressive offloading/quantization, and the **Tencent Hunyuan 3D Community License** blocks commercial deployment in PeekaPlate target markets by explicitly excluding the **European Union, United Kingdom, and South Korea** and capping usage at **1 million Monthly Active Users (MAU)**.

### Cited Findings

#### Comparison vs. `Hunyuan3D-2mini` and `TRELLIS.2-4B` in PeekaPlate
- PeekaPlate's prior Kaggle `2× Tesla T4` (`14.56 GiB`, `sm_75`) benchmark ran `Hunyuan3D-2mini` (`Tencent/Hunyuan3D-2` @ `f8db63096c8282cb27354314d896feba5ba6ff8a`, `tencent/Hunyuan3D-2mini` subfolder `hunyuan3d-dit-v2-mini-fast`, `0.6B` parameters), producing **geometry-only** GLBs (`mc_algo="mc"`, `4.08 GiB` peak VRAM, `68.6–83.1s` per dish) that yielded thin relief (`Z=0.176`) on `03_kimchi` at high camera angles and lacked any texture or PBR maps — [PeekaPlate Dish 3D Delivery Research](file:///home/yacine105/t3-code-handoff/peekaplate/docs/research/dish-3d-delivery.md#L106-L166), [Tencent-Hunyuan/Hunyuan3D-2](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hunyuan3D-2).
- By contrast, full `Hunyuan3D-2.1` splits generation into a two-stage pipeline: a `~3.3B` Diffusion Transformer (`Hunyuan3D-DiT-v2-1` / `hunyuan3d-dit-v2-1`) for watertight SDF mesh generation, followed by a multi-view PBR texture diffusion pipeline (`Hunyuan3D-PaintPBR-v2-1` / `hunyuan3d-paintpbr-v2-1`) — [Tencent-Hunyuan/Hunyuan3D-2.1](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hunyuan3D-2.1), [Hunyuan3D 2.1 Paper (arXiv:2506.15442)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.15442).
- `Hunyuan3D-PaintPBR-v2-1` is grounded in the Disney Principled BRDF model using DINOv2-giant conditioning, a parallel dual-branch UNet, and 3D-Aware RoPE to synthesize multi-view aligned **Albedo**, **Metallic-Roughness**, and **Normal** maps (plus position maps in `hy3dpaint`) across up to `max_num_view=6` at `512` resolution — [Tencent-Hunyuan/Hunyuan3D-2.1](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hunyuan3D-2.1), [Hunyuan3D 2.1 Paper (arXiv:2506.15442)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.15442).
- PeekaPlate's baseline `microsoft/TRELLIS.2-4B` uses a `4B` flow-matching transformer over field-free **O-Voxel** sparse structures and Dual Contouring remeshing (`to_glb`), jointly generating geometry plus PBR channels (`Base Color`, `Roughness`, `Metallic`, and `Opacity`) capable of open/thin surfaces and non-manifold topology (`>=24 GB` NVIDIA GPU memory recommended; `5.16 GiB` single-GPU peak in V20 or `2.78 + 2.71 GiB` dual-T4 FP32 sharded at `512` resolution in V31, though `512` with `remesh_project=0.9` pinched thin curled kimchi folds into `11,059` non-manifold edges) — [microsoft/TRELLIS.2-4B](https://huggingface.co/microsoft/TRELLIS.2-4B), [PeekaPlate Dish 3D Delivery Research](file:///home/yacine105/t3-code-handoff/peekaplate/docs/research/dish-3d-delivery.md#L69-L299).

### Inferences
- **Sequential `2× Tesla T4 (15 GB)` feasibility for `Hunyuan3D-2.1`:** Stage 1 (`Hunyuan3D-DiT-v2-1` at `~10–11.5 GB`) fits comfortably on one `14.56 GiB` T4, and freeing GPU 0 / passing the mesh to GPU 1 avoids simultaneous residency (`29 GB`). However, Stage 2 (`Hunyuan3D-PaintPBR-v2-1` at `~21 GB` for 6 views at `512px`) **exceeds** a single `15 GB` T4 by `~6 GB`, so sequential execution on `2× T4` would fail OOM unless `--low_vram_mode` CPU offloading, reduced view counts/resolution, or community FP8/INT8 quantization (`MMGP`/`Quanto`) are applied with severe latency penalties. By contrast, a `24 GB` cloud GPU (`L4`, `A10G`, `RTX 4090`) can execute Shape (`10 GB`) and PaintPBR (`21 GB`) sequentially with model unloading, while `48 GB+` (`A100 40/80GB`, `L40S`, `H200`) runs the combined `29 GB` pipeline without offloading.
- **Glossy food (`03_kimchi`) & thin plate rims vs. `TRELLIS.2-4B`:** Because `Hunyuan3D-DiT-v2-1` extracts watertight SDF iso-surfaces (`Marching Cubes` / `DMesh`), it tends to avoid `TRELLIS.2-4B`'s non-manifold pinch artifacts on thin curled kimchi edges (seen in PeekaPlate V31 at `512` resolution), while `PaintPBR` directly separates diffuse `Albedo` from specular `Roughness`/`Metallic` so chili oil/sauce glaze can render glossy without baking fixed highlights into base color. Conversely, watertight SDFs can thicken or over-smooth paper-thin plate rims and hollow bowls unless conditioned at high resolution (`2mv` multi-view helps here, whereas `TRELLIS.2-4B` O-Voxel represents open thin shells natively at `1024/1536_cascade`).
- **Legal blocker for PeekaPlate:** Even though `Hunyuan3D-2.1` is technically compelling on `24–48 GB` cloud GPUs, the EU/UK/South Korea exclusion prohibits production deployment for any UK/EU diners or restaurants (and South Korea, notable given the Korean dish benchmarks `02_tteokbokki`, `03_kimchi`, `04_gimbap`), restricting it to private non-EU/UK/KR R&D or requiring a commercial license negotiation with Tencent.

## 2. ByteDance / Stable-X Hi3DGen

### Takeaway
`Hi3DGen` (`Stable-X/Stable3DGen`, `Stable-X/trellis-normal-v0-1`) replaces RGB-to-3D conditioning with a **normal-bridging** pipeline (`NiRNE` normal estimation + `NoRLD` normal-regularized TRELLIS latent diffusion trained on `700k` `DetailVerse` assets) under a clean **MIT License** with proprietary NVIDIA dependencies (`kaolin`, `nvdiffrast`, `flexicubes`) stripped out. However, `Hi3DGen` is strictly **geometry-only** (no native albedo or PBR texture generator) and targets `~17–24 GB` VRAM (`8–12 GB` only with aggressive optimization/reduced resolution), meaning it solves geometric relief (`03_kimchi` folds) better than `Hunyuan3D-2mini` but cannot replace `TRELLIS.2-4B` without pairing a separate commercial-safe multi-view PBR painter.

### Cited Findings
- **Official Repositories & Weights:** Research code and deployments live under `Stable-X/Stable3DGen` on GitHub (`Stable-X/ComfyUI-Hi3DGen` for ComfyUI) and `Stable-X/Hi3DGen` Hugging Face Space; weights include `Stable-X/trellis-normal-v0-1` (normal-conditioned TRELLIS adaptation) and `yoso-normal` (e.g., `yoso-normal-v1-8-1` within the StableNormal/NiRNE pipeline), co-authored by researchers from CUHK (Shenzhen), ByteDance, and Tsinghua University — [Stable-X/Stable3DGen](https://github.com/Stable-X/Stable3DGen), [Stable-X/trellis-normal-v0-1](https://huggingface.co/Stable-X/trellis-normal-v0-1), [Stable-X/Hi3DGen Space](https://huggingface.co/spaces/Stable-X/Hi3DGen).
- **Normal-Bridging Architecture:**
  - **`NiRNE` (Noise-Injected Regressive Normal Estimator):** Applies noise injection and dual-stream training to decouple low-frequency global structure from high-frequency surface edges, generating sharp intermediate surface normal maps from a single RGB image — [Stable-X/Stable3DGen](https://github.com/Stable-X/Stable3DGen).
  - **`NoRLD` (Normal-Regularized Latent Diffusion):** Adapts Microsoft TRELLIS's sparse latent diffusion architecture, regularizing and conditioning 3D generation on the estimated normal map rather than raw RGB pixels — [Stable-X/Stable3DGen](https://github.com/Stable-X/Stable3DGen).
  - **`DetailVerse` Dataset:** Trained on `~700k` synthesized high-detail 3D assets generated via text prompts and `Flux.1-Dev` with pose/quality filtering — [Stable-X/Stable3DGen](https://github.com/Stable-X/Stable3DGen).
- **VRAM Footprint:** Workstation setups typically recommend **`24 GB` VRAM** (`64 GB` system RAM) for unconstrained high-resolution runs (`spconv`, `xformers`, `BiRefNet`, `DINOv2`, `NiRNE`, `NoRLD`), while optimized/alternative setups report **`~17 GB` peak free VRAM**, and community low-VRAM/FP16 configurations can run down to **`8–12 GB` VRAM** at slower speeds — [Stable-X/Stable3DGen](https://github.com/Stable-X/Stable3DGen), [localclaw.io](https://localclaw.io).
- **Texturing Workflow:** `Hi3DGen` is **geometry-only**, outputting untextured meshes (`.glb`, `.obj`, `.ply`, `.stl`); color/PBR texture synthesis must be delegated to a downstream multi-view painter/baker — [Stable-X/Stable3DGen](https://github.com/Stable-X/Stable3DGen).
- **License & Dependencies:** Released under the permissive **MIT License**, with proprietary/restrictive NVIDIA libraries (`kaolin`, `nvdiffrast`, `flexicubes`) explicitly removed in `Stable3DGen` to preserve commercial usability — [Stable-X/Stable3DGen](https://github.com/Stable-X/Stable3DGen).

### Inferences
- **Why normal-bridging helps and hurts on `03_kimchi`:** Passing raw glossy RGB directly to a 3D diffusion model conflates wet specular highlights and shadows with shape depth (`TRELLIS.2-4B` or `Hunyuan3D-2mini` can sink or flatten dark glossy regions). `NiRNE` explicitly separates surface orientation from shading, recovering crisp leaf ridges, chili-flake bumps, and plate rim curvature. Conversely, if wet kimchi oil creates sharp specular reflections on a smooth cabbage surface, `NiRNE` can misinterpret highlight boundaries as false geometric ridges—and because `Hi3DGen` discards color entirely after normal estimation, it cannot produce edible appearance or glossy roughness without a separate texture stage.
- **GPU deployment fit:** A `17–24 GB` peak puts unconstrained `Hi3DGen` above a single `15 GB` T4 (requiring low-VRAM offloading on T4), whereas a `24 GB` `L4`/`A10G`/`RTX 4090` runs `Hi3DGen` comfortably with room for a lightweight downstream texture model sequentially.

### Gaps
- Could not confirm whether a separate `bytedance/Hi3DGen` GitHub repository currently exists or redirects to `Stable-X/Stable3DGen` (only `Stable-X/Stable3DGen` and `Stable-X/Hi3DGen` Space were directly resolved in search outputs).
- Exact official per-stage VRAM table inside `Stable-X/Stable3DGen` README (`17 GB` comes from repo issue/environment reports; exact `NiRNE` vs `NoRLD` parameter counts were also not extracted verbatim).
- Whether `Stable-X/Stable3DGen` ships a default `BiRefNet` (MIT) vs gated/NC background remover in its preprocessing script was not verified line-by-line.

## 3. StepFun Step1X-3D

### Takeaway
`Step1X-3D` (`stepfun-ai/Step1X-3D`) pairs a `1.3B` hybrid TSDF VAE-DiT geometry generator (`Step1X-3D-Geometry-1300m`) with an **SDXL-based** multi-view texture synthesizer (`Step1X-3D-Texture`) under an **Apache-2.0** repo license. However, it consumes **`27–29 GB` VRAM** natively (`~152s` for 50 steps), outputs visual **RGB/albedo-aligned textures rather than full multi-channel PBR maps (metallic/roughness)**, and relies on an **SDXL backbone** whose upstream weights (`stabilityai/stable-diffusion-xl-base-1.0`) carry **CreativeML Open RAIL++-M** restrictions—making it heavier and less PBR-capable than `TRELLIS.2-4B` and incompatible with `2× T4 (15 GB)` without multi-stage offloading.

### Cited Findings
- **GitHub & Hugging Face Repos:** Official repository at `stepfun-ai/Step1X-3D` and weights under `huggingface.co/stepfun-ai` (`Step1X-3D-Geometry-1300m`, `Step1X-3D-Geometry-Label-1300m`, `Step1X-3D-Texture`, plus a curated 2M-asset subset from 5M processed 3D assets) — [stepfun-ai/Step1X-3D](https://github.com/stepfun-ai/Step1X-3D), [stepfun-ai Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/stepfun-ai).
- **Two-Stage Architecture:**
  - **Stage 1 (`Step1X-3D-Geometry-1300m` / `Step1X-3D-Geometry-Label-1300m`):** `1.3B`-parameter hybrid VAE-DiT model with perceiver-based latent encoding and sharp-edge sampling generating watertight **TSDF** (Truncated Signed Distance Function) meshes; the `-Label` variant accepts explicit symmetry/edge-sharpness conditioning labels — [stepfun-ai/Step1X-3D](https://github.com/stepfun-ai/Step1X-3D), [stepfun-ai Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/stepfun-ai).
  - **Stage 2 (`Step1X-3D-Texture`):** Multi-view texture synthesis and baking module built on an **SDXL (Stable Diffusion XL)** backbone using geometry-guided conditioning and latent-space synchronization, enabling 2D LoRA transfer to 3D texturing — [stepfun-ai/Step1X-3D](https://github.com/stepfun-ai/Step1X-3D).
- **Official VRAM & Latency Table (`stepfun-ai/Step1X-3D`):**
  - `Step1X-3D-Geometry-1300m + Step1X-3D-Texture`: **~27 GB VRAM**, **~152s** (50 steps) — [stepfun-ai/Step1X-3D](https://github.com/stepfun-ai/Step1X-3D).
  - `Step1X-3D-Geometry-Label-1300m + Step1X-3D-Texture`: **~29 GB VRAM**, **~152s** (50 steps) — [stepfun-ai/Step1X-3D](https://github.com/stepfun-ai/Step1X-3D).
  - Recommended local GPU floor for un-offloaded inference: **`32 GB` VRAM** (`64 GB` RAM) — [localclaw.io](https://localclaw.io).
- **Geometry vs. Texture / PBR Fidelity:** Stage 1 emphasizes sharp, watertight TSDF geometry, while Stage 2 concentrates on cross-view visual RGB/albedo alignment rather than joint physically-based material decomposition (`Metallic`/`Roughness` maps are absent or minimal out of the box compared to `Hunyuan3D-2.1` or `TRELLIS.2-4B`) — [stepfun-ai/Step1X-3D](https://github.com/stepfun-ai/Step1X-3D).
- **License & Upstream Backbones:** `stepfun-ai/Step1X-3D` is licensed under **Apache License 2.0**, and uses **DINOv2** (Apache-2.0), **SDXL** (`stabilityai/stable-diffusion-xl-base-1.0`), and acknowledges `BiRefNet`, `FLUX`, `MV-Adapter`, and `Hunyuan3D 2.0` in its ecosystem/preprocessing stack — [stepfun-ai/Step1X-3D](https://github.com/stepfun-ai/Step1X-3D).

### Inferences
- **Commercial license caveat on SDXL dependency:** Although StepFun's repository license is Apache-2.0, `Step1X-3D-Texture` builds atop SDXL (`CreativeML Open RAIL++-M` use-based restrictions) and optional preprocessing/ecosystem components (`BiRefNet` MIT, `Hunyuan3D-2.0` Community License if invoked). Legal review should verify whether `Step1X-3D-Texture` weights remain governed solely by Apache-2.0 or inherit SDXL Open RAIL++-M restrictions.
- **Dish photo suitability & GPU fit:** `27–29 GB` native VRAM disqualifies un-offloaded execution on `15 GB` T4 and exceeds `24 GB` (`L4`/`A10G`/`RTX 4090`) unless Stage 1 (`Geometry`) is unloaded before Stage 2 (`Texture`). Moreover, lacking native `Roughness`/`Metallic` PBR channels makes `Step1X-3D` weaker than `TRELLIS.2-4B` or `Hunyuan3D-2.1` for wet/glossy dishes like `03_kimchi` or `02_tteokbokki` glaze, where baked SDXL highlights look flat or chalky under interactive studio lighting.

### Gaps
- Exact standalone VRAM footprints for Stage 1 (`Step1X-3D-Geometry-1300m`) alone vs. Stage 2 (`Step1X-3D-Texture`) alone, and whether official scripts include a `--low_vram` / sequential CPU offload flag fitting within `16–24 GB`, were not confirmed from the official README summary (which reports combined `27 GB` / `29 GB` figures).
- Exact parameter count of `Step1X-3D-Texture` and exact Hugging Face sub-repo IDs (`stepfun-ai/Step1X-3D` vs separate sub-repos) were not enumerated completely.

## 4. VAST AI TripoSG

### Takeaway
`TripoSG` (`VAST-AI-Research/TripoSG`) is a `1.5B`-parameter rectified flow SDF Diffusion Transformer using `2048` latent tokens under an **MIT License** and fitting comfortably in **`>=8 GB` VRAM** (`FP16`), making it dramatically sharper in geometry than legacy `TripoSR` and immediately runnable on a single `15 GB` Tesla T4. However, `TripoSG` is **strictly geometry-only** (no native texture or PBR generator) and ships with **`briaai/RMBG-1.4` (strictly non-commercial)** as its default background remover—requiring PeekaPlate to keep its own MIT/Apache segmentation (`birefnet-general-lite` + `GroundingDINO` + `SAM`) and pair `TripoSG` with an external commercial-safe texture pipeline (such as `MV-Adapter` + `TEXGen`, both Apache-2.0).

### Cited Findings
- **Official Repositories:** Code on GitHub at `VAST-AI-Research/TripoSG` and weights on Hugging Face at `VAST-AI/TripoSG` (plus variant `TripoSG-scribble`) — [VAST-AI-Research/TripoSG](https://github.com/VAST-AI-Research/TripoSG), [VAST-AI/TripoSG](https://huggingface.co/VAST-AI/TripoSG).
- **Architecture vs. Legacy `TripoSR`:**
  - `TripoSG` replaces `TripoSR`'s feed-forward Large Reconstruction Model (LRM) triplane NeRF with a **`1.5B` parameter rectified flow Diffusion Transformer** paired with a **3D VAE** operating at **`2048` latent tokens** and trained with hybrid geometric supervision (**SDF, normal, and eikonal losses**) on a large-scale curated 3D dataset — [VAST-AI-Research/TripoSG](https://github.com/VAST-AI-Research/TripoSG), [VAST-AI/TripoSG](https://huggingface.co/VAST-AI/TripoSG).
  - By comparison, `TripoSR` (already tested in PeekaPlate at `2.323 GiB` VRAM, `~10s/dish`) outputs coarse marching-cubes geometry (`60k–163k` faces) with **vertex colors only** (`COLOR_0`), whereas `TripoSG` synthesizes cleaner, higher-detail SDF meshes (`2048` tokens vs feed-forward triplane regression) — [PeekaPlate Dish 3D Delivery Research](file:///home/yacine105/t3-code-handoff/peekaplate/docs/research/dish-3d-delivery.md#L106-L229), [VAST-AI-Research/TripoSG](https://github.com/VAST-AI-Research/TripoSG).
- **VRAM Requirement:** Official repository requires a CUDA GPU with **`>=8 GB` VRAM** using `FP16` mixed precision and memory-conscious hierarchical decoding — [VAST-AI-Research/TripoSG](https://github.com/VAST-AI-Research/TripoSG).
- **Texturing Support:** `TripoSG` generates **untextured `.glb` geometry only**; VAST/community issues (`#59`, `#65`) confirm texture/UV generation must be supplied externally via multi-view/UV painters such as **`MV-Adapter`** (`huanngzh/MV-Adapter`, **Apache-2.0**), **`TEXGen`** (`Andyx/TEXGen`, **Apache-2.0**), or `Hunyuan3D-Paint` — [VAST-AI-Research/TripoSG](https://github.com/VAST-AI-Research/TripoSG), [huanngzh/MV-Adapter](https://huggingface.co/huanngzh/MV-Adapter), [Andyx/TEXGen](https://huggingface.co/Andyx/TEXGen).
- **License & Preprocessing Blocker:** Core `TripoSG` code and weights are **MIT Licensed**, **but** its inference setup automatically downloads **`briaai/RMBG-1.4`** into `pretrained_weights/RMBG-1.4` for background removal, which is governed by a **strictly Non-Commercial license (`bria-rmbg-1.4` / CC BY-NC)** — [VAST-AI-Research/TripoSG](https://github.com/VAST-AI-Research/TripoSG), [briaai/RMBG-1.4](https://huggingface.co/briaai/RMBG-1.4).

### Inferences
- **Practical PeekaPlate fit:** Because `TripoSG` fits in `~8–12 GB` VRAM in `FP16`, two dishes can run in parallel across `2× Tesla T4 (15 GB)`—just like PeekaPlate's prior `TripoSR` / `Hunyuan3D-2mini` dual-T4 setup—or sequentially alongside `MV-Adapter` + `TEXGen` without touching Tencent's EU/UK/KR-excluded `Hunyuan3D-Paint`. However, PeekaPlate **must bypass `RMBG-1.4`** entirely and feed its existing MIT/Apache plate-preserving `cutout_rgba.png` (`birefnet-general-lite` + `GroundingDINO` + `SAM`), and `TripoSG` + external painter will still lack native joint geometry-material PBR (`Roughness`/`Metallic`/`Opacity`) co-generation unless `TEXGen`/downstream shading explicitly synthesizes PBR channels.

### Gaps
- Could not verify exact per-dish inference latency (`seconds`) or exact peak VRAM in `GiB` when `TripoSG` runs paired with `MV-Adapter` + `TEXGen` on a `15 GB` T4.
- Did not inspect `VAST-AI/TripoSG-scribble` or verify whether any unofficial community `TripoSG` texture branch produces PBR vs RGB-only textures.

## 5. Other Notable Open-Weights Contenders & TRELLIS.2-4B Comparison Matrix

### Takeaway
None of the secondary open-weights contenders (`SPAR3D`, `SF3D`, `Direct3D-S2`, `Unique3D`, `TripoSF`, `CraftsMan3D`) simultaneously match `TRELLIS.2-4B`'s open-topology O-Voxel Dual Contouring **and** full joint PBR material generation (`Base Color`, `Roughness`, `Metallic`, `Opacity`) without license or hardware blockers. `SPAR3D` (`~10.5 GB` / `7 GB` low-VRAM) and `SF3D` (`~6 GB`) are the only lightweight native PBR alternatives for `T4` (`15 GB`), but both carry Stability AI's **$1M annual revenue threshold** and rely on fast regressive/single-image estimation that tends to soften complex occluded food geometry (`03_kimchi`), while `Direct3D-S2` (`MIT`, up to `2B`, `1024^3` sparse SDF) is geometry-only and requires Ampere+ (`RTX 30xx+`) FlashAttention—making it incompatible with Tesla T4 (`sm_75`).

### Inferences
- **Organic & plated food topology (`03_kimchi`) vs. `TRELLIS.2-4B`:** None of the MIT/Apache alternatives cleanly beat `TRELLIS.2-4B` end-to-end on *both* open/layered food topology and joint PBR materials. `SPAR3D`/`SF3D` deliver fast PBR on `T4` but rely on regressive global roughness/metallic prediction and lower-detail backside synthesis, risking melted/blobby kimchi stacks; `Direct3D-S2` and `TripoSF` advance high-res geometry (`1024^3`) without texturing; `Unique3D`'s multi-view blending struggles with thin overlapping food cavities without PBR specularity separation.
- **Strategic takeaway for PeekaPlate:** On `24–48 GB` cloud GPUs (`A100`/`L40S`/`H200`/`4090`), `TRELLIS.2-4B` (`1536_cascade` `bf16` with reviewed RGBA cutout bypassing `RMBG-2.0`) remains the most license-clean and topology/PBR-capable single-image open-weights model, with `Hi3DGen` (`MIT`) + `MV-Adapter`/`TEXGen` (`Apache-2.0`) as the strongest fully permissive geometry-first fallback if `TRELLIS.2-4B` `DINOv3` gating remains a blocker, and `Hunyuan3D-2.1` disqualified from EU/UK/KR commercial serving by Tencent's territorial clause.

### Gaps
- Exact per-model parameter counts for `SPAR3D`, `SF3D`, `Unique3D`, and `TripoSF`, as well as measured empirical benchmarks on real plated food photos (`01_pizza`..`04_gimbap`), were not available in primary docs and remain unverified without execution.
- `DreamTechAI/TEXTRIX` / `MIKU` (mentioned alongside `Direct3D-S2` as DreamTech's texture/PBR repos) were not deeply audited for standalone license, VRAM, or compatibility with `Direct3D-S2` meshes.
